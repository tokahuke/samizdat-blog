# About us

SAMIZDAT is a free software initiative from me, [a guy you just met on the Internet](https://github.com/tokahuke) and some more helpful folks you have not met yet, but are as important. I am of course talking about the contributors to the project. Do you want to join the cause? Anyone is welcome, as long as you keep the conversation civil. Go to the [project page on GitHub](https://github.com/tokahuke/samizdat) and have a look around. There is plenty to be done!

No idea how to code? No problem! You can also [donate some crypto](/donate/) to be used in the project. We run a public testbed at `testbed.hubfederation.com` (the seed hub that every fresh install connects to by default) and a web proxy at `proxy.hubfederation.com` (so people without a node can still view Samizdat-published sites). Those boxes are not free, and with your support we can also lure developers to work for the project with the sweet scent of cash.

## Why does Samizdat exist?

Four goals, in order of how settled each one is:

1. **Decentralised static hosting.** Allow one to serve a public, static site
   without paying a hosting service. The content lives on the publisher's own
   device and in caches on the devices of people who visit the site.
2. **Content-addressed URLs.** Give resources on this network a URL scheme
   that names what they are, not where they sit. No registrar, no DNS, no
   platform you can be deplatformed from.
3. **Oblivious hosting.** Only the device serving the content and the device
   asking for it should learn anything about the content or its metadata. The
   hubs that broker the connection see only opaque-encrypted traffic.
4. **Ease and convenience.** All of the above, but with graphical interfaces,
   mobile apps, and the amenities of a well-built tool. We are not there yet,
   but we want to be.

## User privacy

No, we have no data on anything. We track no one, there is no database on anything, no accounts, no IPs, no cookies, no timestamps. And thank God! Those things are a pain to maintain and cost money. If you have a warrant, we will be glad to take a look. However, you are most likely to come out empty-handed.

We just make free software for anyone to use, [without any warranty](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.html).
