The SAMIZDAT SYSTEM

Your content, available.

SAMIZDAT is a distributed self-publishing system. Publish and view content you love on the Internet without meddling intermediaries!

Get Samizdat View on GitHub

All Samizdat Software is Free Software and is fully available free of charge.

How it works

  1. 1

    Build your site locally, in your own computer.

  2. 2

    Publish to the Samizdat network, a peer-to-peer content distribution system.

  3. 3

    People who visit your site will propagate your ideas further, using their cache.

What's different

No registrar. No DNS. No platform.

The upside

Convenient

Forget about looking for platforms to serve your content. Deploy locally and let your biggest fans serve it for you.

Private

Only the parties serving and receiving content know what it is all about. No corporation-in-the-middle; no government-in-the-middle.

Uncensorable

No central kill-switch. Your content sits on the disks of people who chose to host it. There is no platform to deplatform you from.

Free

Free Software. AGPLv3 licensed. No fee, no rent, no enterprise tier; just download and go.

It's just the Web all over again. But think of the possibilities!

By structuring the Web on content rather than on location, we bypass the the big servers and the big companies. Your content is not on Google or on Meta; it's with your audience.
